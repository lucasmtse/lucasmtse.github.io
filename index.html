About

Who I am

I combine a solid background in statistics & econometrics with hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning, and data visualization, both in academic and real-world projects.

My name is Lucas, I'm 23 years old and I've lived in Toulouse my entire life. I studied for five years at the Toulouse School of Economics where I completed a Master's degree in Statistics & Econometrics. It was during these years that I discovered machine learning, and from the very first courses, I developed a strong interest for predictive modelling and statistical learning.

In my second year of the Master's program, I was introduced to artificial neural networks and quickly became fascinated by their ability to learn complex patterns. This curiosity naturally led me to focus on deep learning and machine learning, and I had the opportunity to carry out my internship on convolutional neural networks (CNNs), a family of Neural Networks specifically designed for image analysis.

Outside of data and ML, I'm someone who loves staying active. I practice skiing and surfing whenever I can, and I regularly go to the gym, it keeps me grounded and motivated. I also enjoy reading, especially books or papers that help me stay curious, open-minded, and inspired.

On the side, I keep developing various personal projects on GitHub: Formula 1 analytics with APIs, anomaly detection for fraud, plates detection with YOLO, and more. I like building clean, well-documented, reproducible code, and I'm always looking for new ideas to sharpen my skills and explore different areas of Data Science and Machine Learning.
